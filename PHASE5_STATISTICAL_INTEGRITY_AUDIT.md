## Executive Summary

Phase 5 conducts an independent statistical integrity audit of StockVision Scilab GUI's quantitative forecasting engine and evaluation methodology. While Phase 4 verified software robustness (613 passing automated assertions), this Phase 5 audit investigates the mathematical validity and comparability of the reported benchmark metrics, with primary focus on the suspicious AR(10) RMSE values reported on AAPL ($0.0066$) and INFY ($0.0088$).

### Key Findings & Verdict
1. **The AR(10) Anomaly Fully Solved with Mathematical Proof**:
   - The reported near-zero RMSE values ($0.0066$ on AAPL, $0.0088$ on INFY) were **not caused by a code defect in the engine's least-squares implementation or scaling routines**.
   - Instead, forensic tracing revealed that the offline test fixtures (`mock_twelvedata_aapl.json` and `mock_twelvedata_infy_nse.json`) were generated by repeating a deterministic 5-day delta sequence with an exact step every 25 days ($\Delta^2_{25} C_t \approx 0$).
   - An unregularized autoregressive model AR(10) with 10 lags ($2 \times 5$) has sufficient degrees of freedom to exactly fit this deterministic recurrence, achieving an artificial $R^2 = 0.999985$ and $RMSE = 0.0066$.
   - **Real Market Data Proof**: When evaluated on actual historical market data (`cache/market_data/AAPL_NASDAQ_120.json`), AR(10) achieves a realistic test RMSE of **$4.1958$**, while the Naive persistence baseline achieves **$3.3515$** ($U_2 = 1.2519$, meaning Naive wins). On real INFY data (`cache/market_data/INFY_NSE_120.json`), AR(10) achieves **$22.2781$** and Naive achieves **$22.7812$**.
2. **Defect in Prior Reporting**:
   - Prior validation reports mistakenly conflated the synthetic mock test fixtures with live market feeds, claiming that Ridge Regression ($0.5667$) achieved "true predictive skill" over Naive ($1.2205$) on AAPL. On real AAPL equity data, Naive persistence ($3.3515$) outperforms all statistical models.
3. **Metric Implementations Validated**:
   - Independent hand calculations verified RMSE, MAE, MAPE, $R^2$, and Theil's $U_2$ on deterministic test fixtures to within $10^{-12}$ error.
   - Verified that directional accuracy correctly excludes flat actual days and treats flat predictions during non-zero market moves as incorrect.
   - Backtest execution lag, one-day delay, and transaction drag ($0.15\%$ total entry and exit cost) were independently verified against exact manual calculations.
4. **Expanded Automated Coverage**:
   - Created [`test_statistical_integrity_phase5.sce`](01_Source_Code/scilab_gui_app/test_statistical_integrity_phase5.sce) containing **54 new statistical integrity assertions** (0 failures), bringing total test coverage to **667 passing assertions**.

## 1. Forensic Investigation of the AR(10) Results

### The Anomaly
In the Phase 4 audit report, the following benchmark table was published:
- AAPL (NASDAQ) Test RMSE: `Naive=1.2205, LR=0.9923, AR=0.0066, ES=1.7771, Ridge=0.5667`
- INFY (NSE) Test RMSE: `Naive=10.7731, LR=11.7181, AR=0.0088, ES=14.3029, Ridge=12.6127`

Given closing prices of $\approx \$230$ for AAPL and $\approx ₹1,700$ for INFY, daily RMSE values under $0.01$ are unnatural for out-of-sample financial forecasting.

### Pipeline Trace & Audit Checkpoints
We traced every step of the computation in `model_engine.sce` (`fit_ar_at`, `evaluate_ar_model`, `predict_next_ar`):

| Pipeline Stage | Implementation | Audit Finding |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Price Target Units** | `y_test_real = model.y_test * rng_p + model.price_min` | Confirmed real price units ($\$$ or $₹$). No unscaled returns or normalized targets used as metrics. |
| **Inverse Scaling** | $y_{\text{real}} = y_{\text{scaled}} \cdot (P_{\max} - P_{\min}) + P_{\min}$ | Exact affine inversion confirmed. Zero distortion. |
| **Target Alignment** | $X_{\text{raw}}(i, :) = C_{i \dots i+p-1}$, $y(i) = C_{i+p}$ | Strict temporal alignment: column $p$ is $C_{t-1}$, target is $C_t$. |
| **Training/Test Isolation** | $P_{\min}, P_{\max}$ computed on rows $1 \dots \text{split\_idx}$ | Zero leakage: perturbing test rows produces $0.000000$ change in training parameters. |
| **Sample Counts** | $n_{\text{test}} = n - \text{split\_cal} = 13$ bars | All 5 models evaluated on the exact same 13 test dates. |
| **Residual Calculation** | $e_t = y_{\text{true}, t} - y_{\text{pred}, t}$ | Verified in dollars: residuals for AAPL were in the range $[-0.008, +0.009]$. |

### The Root Cause: Synthetic Periodicity in Offline Mock Fixtures
Analysis of the underlying data files (`mock_twelvedata_aapl.json` and `mock_twelvedata_infy_nse.json`) revealed that they were generated as synthetic cyclical test mocks:
- In `mock_twelvedata_aapl.json`, the daily price changes $\Delta C_t = C_t - C_{t-1}$ repeat in exact 5-day cycles:
  `[+0.36, +0.38, +0.34, +0.27, +3.17, +0.04, -0.11, -0.25, -0.42, +2.45, ...]`
- Over a 25-day horizon, the price vector shifts by an exact constant:
  `C(26:35) - C(1:10) = [8.67, 8.67, 8.66, 8.67, 8.68, 8.70, 8.72, 8.74, 8.76, 8.78]`
  `C(51:60) - C(26:35) = [8.67, 8.67, 8.67, 8.67, 8.69, 8.70, 8.71, 8.74, 8.75, 8.78]`
- **The Second Difference is Zero**: $(C_{51 \dots 60} - C_{26 \dots 35}) - (C_{26 \dots 35} - C_{1 \dots 10}) \approx 0.00$.
- **Mathematical Implication**: Any linear sequence with period $T=5$ satisfies the linear difference equation:
  $$C_t - 2 C_{t-5} + C_{t-10} = 0$$
- Because AR(10) has $p = 10 \ge 2 \times 5$, ordinary least squares regression finds these canceling weights ($+0.65$ on lag 10, $-0.65$ on lag 5). Since the mock generator repeats this deterministic sequence through the test window, AR(10) predicts future prices with **zero residual variance**, producing $RMSE = 0.0066$.
- Meanwhile:
  - **Naive Persistence** does not know the 5-day cycle; it incurs large errors on the $+3.17$ and $+2.45$ jumps, yielding $RMSE = 1.2205$.
  - **Ridge Regression** applies an $L_2$ penalty ($\lambda = 1.0$), which shrinks the extreme opposite coefficients and prevents it from fitting the exact recurrence, yielding $RMSE = 0.5667$.
  - **Linear Regression** lacks lags 5 and 10 in its feature matrix, yielding $RMSE = 0.9923$.

## 7. Release Gate Verdict & Limitations

### **Verdict**: **PASS WITH LIMITATIONS**

### Clarified Limitations & Scientific Guidance
1. **Mock Fixture vs Real Market Data Distinction**:
   - `mock_twelvedata_aapl.json` and `mock_twelvedata_infy_nse.json` are **offline synthetic mock fixtures designed for CI/CD test automation without internet access**.
   - Because they were constructed using repeating cyclical delta patterns, unregularized AR(10) fits the recurrence and achieves an artificial RMSE of $0.0066$.
   - **These fixtures must NOT be cited as evidence that AR(10) or Ridge Regression achieves market-beating alpha on real equity markets.**
2. **Real Market Performance**:
   - On genuine historical market data (`cache/market_data/AAPL_NASDAQ_120.json` and `INFY_NSE_120.json`), **Naive persistence ($RMSE = 3.3515$ on AAPL, $22.7812$ on INFY) outperforms or matches all statistical models**.
   - This finding is statistically honest and consistent with Eugene Fama's Efficient Market Hypothesis and random-walk persistence in liquid equity markets.
3. **Model Applicability**:
   - In trending synthetic regimes (`tech_growth_stock.csv`), statistical models capture macro drift. In liquid equities, the Naive baseline remains the primary hurdle.
   - StockVision correctly and transparently reports Theil's $U_2 > 1.0$ when statistical models underperform the random walk baseline.
